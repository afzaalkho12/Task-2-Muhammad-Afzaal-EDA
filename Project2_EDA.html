Objective¶

  • Identify months with the highest and lowest order volumes.
  • Understand monthly ordering trends.

Visualization¶

A bar chart is used to compare the number of orders across different months.

Interpretation¶

The bar chart displays the monthly distribution of customer orders. Higher bars indicate months with increased customer activity, while lower bars indicate periods with fewer orders.

Key Insight¶

Business Insights¶

Based on the exploratory data analysis, the following business insights were identified.

Key Business Insights¶

  • Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, indicating seasonal purchasing behavior.

  • A small number of products contribute significantly to total sales.

  • Most customers prefer one or two payment methods over others.

  • The majority of orders are successfully delivered, indicating efficient order fulfillment.

  • Certain referral sources generate more customers, highlighting effective marketing channels.

  • Many customers complete purchases without using coupon codes.

  • Total order value generally increases with higher product prices and larger purchase quantities.

  • A few outliers represent unusually large orders, which may correspond to bulk purchases or premium customers.
